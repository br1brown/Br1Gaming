### Access to the reserved area

If you sign in to the reserved area, the controller processes your username and credentials to recognise you and keep the session active; the session stays in the browser tab and ends when you close it. Every sign-in, successful or failed, is noted in the technical records together with the username typed, even when the attempt fails, to protect accounts from intrusion attempts. Files you upload remain linked to your account: if you delete it, the files remain but are no longer linked to you.

The controller does this to provide the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the sign-in records, because it has a legitimate interest in protecting accounts from unauthorised access (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Account data is kept as long as the account is active, while sign-in records follow the rotation described above. Providing this data is only needed to use the reserved area: without it you can still browse the public pages.
