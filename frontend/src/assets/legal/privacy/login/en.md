### Access to the reserved area

If you sign in to the reserved area, we process your username and credentials to authenticate you and keep the session active. The session stays in the browser tab and ends when you close it. Every sign-in, successful or failed, is recorded in the technical logs with the username, to protect the account from intrusion attempts. Files you upload remain associated with your account; if you delete it, the files remain but are no longer linked to you.

- **Legal basis:** performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for sign-in logs, the controller's legitimate interest in protecting accounts from unauthorised access and intrusion attempts (point (f)).
- **Retention:** as long as the account remains active; sign-in logs follow the rotation described above.
- **Provision:** needed only to use the reserved area: without it you can still browse the public pages.
