# Privacy Policy

This site is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect personal data, we do not track your browsing, and we do not share information with third parties.

The only information processed is strictly necessary to make the site work.

## Camera (Lombroso Scanner)

The "Lombroso Scanner" feature uses your device's camera only for the photo shown on screen. The photo never leaves your browser: it is processed locally to compute a simple numeric value (not a face analysis), the only piece of data sent to the server to get a verdict. The photo is never uploaded, saved, or stored in any way — not by us, nor in cookies or local storage — and is discarded right after use.
