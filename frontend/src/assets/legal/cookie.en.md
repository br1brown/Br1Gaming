## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

If your browser (or an extension) sends the **Global Privacy Control** signal — or another equivalent universal opt-out signal — we automatically recognize it as a request not to sell or share your data: **Analytics** and **Profiling** cookies stay disabled, with no interaction required on your part. The signal does not affect **Technical** cookies, which are necessary for the site to function. You can still change your choice at any time from the cookie management panel below.
