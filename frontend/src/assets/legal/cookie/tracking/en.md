## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

If your browser (or an extension) sends the **Global Privacy Control** signal, or another equivalent universal opt-out signal, the site treats it as a refusal of the tracking cookies listed above, with no action required on your part. The signal does not affect **technical** cookies. You can still change your choice at any time: from the cookie banner until you have made one, then from the cookie management panel further down this page or from the cookie icon button on the other pages.
